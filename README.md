# AstroSage Call & Chat Center Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes AstroSage's call and chat consultation operations using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to identify operational inefficiencies, customer satisfaction trends, revenue drivers, agent/guru performance, workload patterns, and opportunities for improving overall business performance.

The analysis was also used to recommend how a ₹1 crore investment could be allocated to maximize operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

AstroSage wanted to improve its call center operations after receiving a ₹1 crore investment.

The key business questions addressed in this project were:

- How is call volume changing over time?
- Which consultation categories generate the most revenue?
- What are the major call and chat performance issues?
- Which gurus have the highest customer interactions?
- What are the customer satisfaction trends?
- What are the peak call hours?
- How much is being spent on operations?
- How frequently do customers return?
- How should the ₹1 crore investment be allocated?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The dataset was cleaned before analysis.

Major cleaning steps included:

- Checked for duplicate records
- Identified missing and inconsistent values
- Removed unnecessary entries
- Used TRIM to remove extra spaces from guru names
- Replaced applicable blank values with `N/A`
- Standardized TRUE/FALSE values to YES/NO
- Converted relevant fields into appropriate number formats
- Created additional date and time helper columns

## 📈 Key Analysis

### Revenue Analysis

Call consultations were the primary revenue-generating category, followed by chat consultations.

The analysis showed that revenue was highly concentrated in Call and Chat services.

### Call & Chat Performance

Chat performance showed significant operational issues:

- Failed chats: 37.18%
- Incomplete chats: 34.03%
- Completed chats: 28.36%

Call performance showed:

- Completed: 40.56%
- No-answer: 20.31%
- Busy: 14.92%
- Failed: 13.93%
- Incomplete: 10.28%

These results indicate that improving service completion and availability could have a significant impact.

### Customer Satisfaction

The overall average customer rating was approximately 2.93/5.

The analysis also compared customer ratings across platforms, gurus, consultation types, and time periods.

### Call Volume

The analysis identified changes in daily call volume and peak operating hours.

The highest daily call volume recorded in the analysis was 430 calls on 10-Dec.

### Guru Performance

Guru-level analysis was performed to identify high-interaction gurus and compare customer satisfaction across gurus.

### Repeat Customers

The analysis found that repeat calls represented a significant portion of total call activity, highlighting the importance of customer retention and service quality.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Call consultations were the major revenue contributor.
2. Chat services had a high proportion of failed and incomplete interactions.
3. Call demand was concentrated during specific hours of the day.
4. Customer satisfaction had significant room for improvement.
5. A small number of gurus handled a large share of customer interactions.
6. Repeat customers represented a substantial portion of call activity.
7. Improving agent performance and service availability could improve both customer experience and operational efficiency.

## 💰 Investment Recommendation

Based on the analysis, the ₹1 crore investment was recommended to be allocated as follows:

| Area | Investment |
|---|---:|
| Agent Training | ₹40 Lakh |
| Technology Upgrade | ₹30 Lakh |
| Peak-Hour Staffing | ₹20 Lakh |
| Customer Experience | ₹10 Lakh |
| **Total** | **₹1 Crore** |

### 1. Agent Training — ₹40 Lakh

Focus on:

- Customer communication
- Problem solving
- Chat handling
- Training low-rated agents
- Improving service quality

### 2. Technology Upgrade — ₹30 Lakh

Potential improvements include:

- Automatic Call Distribution
- AI chatbot
- AI-based call analytics
- Better call routing
- Performance monitoring

### 3. Peak-Hour Staffing — ₹20 Lakh

Increase agent availability during high-demand periods and introduce callback options to reduce waiting time.

### 4. Customer Experience — ₹10 Lakh

Monitor:

- Customer ratings
- Feedback
- Complaints
- Call quality
- Consultation performance
